Action Space:

Discrete(70)

0 - pass
1 - pick
2 - declare go alone
3 - declare ace clubs
4 - declare ace spaces
5 - declare ace hearts
6-37 - bury card
38-69 - play card

Observation Space:

13x32 MultiBinary with

Row Index:
0 -> Current Hand
1 -> Current Cards Taken
2 -> Opponent 1's Known Cards Taken
3 -> Opponent 2's Known Cards Taken
4 -> Opponent 3's Known Cards Taken
5 -> Opponent 4's Known Cards Taken
6 -> My card played for current trick
7 -> Opponent 1's card for current trick
8 -> Opponent 2's card for current trick
9 -> Opponent 3's card for current trick
10 -> Opponent 4's card for current trick
11 -> Unknown Cards
12 -> Current Suit

Column Index:
Each Card

Along with 4 optional Discrete(32) for the current trick.
And a Discrete(4) for called suit.

            Action 0 - "Pass"
            
            Action 2 - Declare "Go Alone"
            Action 3 - Declare "Ace of Clubs"
            Action 4 - Declare "Ace of Spades"
            Action 5 - Declare "Ace of Hearts"
